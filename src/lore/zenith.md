## OVERVIEW

**Zenith** is the planet humanity settled after abandoning Earth nearly 1,000 years ago. Although humans have lived on Zenith for centuries, much of the planet remains poorly understood.

Zenith is fundamentally different from Earth. Its native ecosystems contain plants, animals, and geological processes that have no direct Earth equivalent. Human science has advanced considerably since settlement, but the scale of the planet, the difficulty of studying its native environments, and humanity's early history on Zenith have left significant gaps in scientific knowledge.

Most of Zenith's human population lives within one of its **24 Zones**, which are heavily developed and environmentally managed regions controlled by the **Monoliths**. Outside them lies the **Pale**, a vast and diverse wilderness that is considerably more difficult for ordinary humans to inhabit.

## GEOGRAPHY & ENVIRONMENT

Zenith is a large and geographically diverse planet with a wide range of climates and ecosystems. Its landscapes include dry plains, deserts, forests, wetlands, mountains, and other environments.

The planet can appear relatively familiar at first glance, particularly near human settlements. Further from the Zones, however, the scale and diversity of Zenith's native environment become increasingly apparent. Large areas of the planet remain sparsely explored or difficult to access.

Zenith's environments are not uniformly hostile to humans. Many parts of the planet support abundant plant and animal life, although conditions can vary significantly between regions.

## NATIVE LIFE

Zenith is home to a vast range of native flora and fauna. Much of its biology remains poorly understood, particularly outside developed regions.

Native organisms can differ dramatically from Earth life in size, behavior, anatomy, and ecological role. Some species are relatively harmless and live alongside human settlements, while others can pose significant dangers to people and infrastructure.

Extreme examples of Zenithian wildlife are not considered supernatural. An organism may be enormous, unusual, or rarely encountered while still being a naturally occurring part of Zenith's ecosystem.

## THE ZONES

The **24 Zones** are the primary centers of human civilization on Zenith. Each Zone is governed by a **Monolith**, a megacorporation that controls its government, infrastructure, and major industries.

Zones are heavily managed environments. Their infrastructure, climate control, sanitation, and other systems allow large populations to live in conditions considered safe and predictable by Zenithan standards.

Each Zone is environmentally separated from its surroundings by a largely invisible **barrier**. The barrier helps maintain the environmental conditions inside the Zone and limits the movement of outside air, microorganisms, contaminants, and environmental destruction.

The barriers are normally difficult to see. Near their edges, however, they become increasingly visible and physically resistant. Leaving a Zone requires pushing through the barrier like a flexible membrane.

The barriers are not intended to function as impenetrable walls. Under the right circumstances, Humans, Flora/Fauna, and other natural elements can cross them.

## HUMAN IMPACT

Human settlement has significantly altered Zenith's environment.

Resource extraction, industrial pollution, deforestation, water diversion, and other forms of development have changed ecosystems across the planet. Earth organisms introduced by humanity have also affected native environments.

The modern Pale is partly a product of these changes. Human activity has contributed to making some regions more difficult for ordinary humans to inhabit, while other organisms and ecosystems have adapted to or benefited from the altered conditions.

This relationship is poorly understood by the general population. Most Zenithans recognize that humanity has damaged parts of the planet, but the connection between human environmental impact and the present condition of the Pale is not widely understood.

The common belief is simply that the environment outside the Zones is naturally dangerous.

## SCIENCE & EXPLORATION

Humanity has spent nearly a thousand years studying Zenith, but significant gaps in scientific knowledge remain.

The planet's enormous size, difficult environments, unfamiliar biology, and incomplete records from humanity's early settlement have all limited research. Scientists continue to document species and ecosystems that have never been studied in detail.

As a result, Zenith contains many environments that are known to exist without being fully understood.
