## OVERVIEW

**Zenith** is the planet humanity settled after abandoning Earth nearly 1,000 years ago.

The planet is significantly different from Earth, with its own plants, animals, ecosystems, and geological processes. Humanity has spent centuries studying Zenith, but the majority of the planet's aspects remain barely understood.

Most of humanity lives within one of the **24 Zones**, heavily developed regions controlled by the **Monoliths**. Outside the Zones lies the **Pale**, where conditions are much harder for humans to live in.

## GEOGRAPHY & LIFE

While appearing on the surface very similar to earth, Zenith is a strange and diverse planet, with many biomes and ecosystems having no direct Earth equivalent.

Some species are harmless and live alongside humans, while others can be dangerous to people and infrastructure.  

Large portions of the planet remain sparsely explored, particularly outside developed areas.

## THE ZONES

The **24 Zones** are the main centers of human civilization on Zenith. Each is governed by a **Monolith**, which controls its government, infrastructure, and major industries.

Zones are heavily managed environments designed to keep their populations safe and comfortable. Each Zone is surrounded by a largely invisible **barrier** that helps maintain its conditions and limits the movement of contaminants and other environmental factors.
