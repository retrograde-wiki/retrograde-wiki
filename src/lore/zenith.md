## OVERVIEW

**Zenith** is the planet humanity settled after abandoning Earth nearly 1,000 years ago.

The planet is significantly different from Earth, with its own plants, animals, ecosystems, and geological processes. Humanity has spent centuries studying Zenith, but large parts of the planet remain poorly understood.

Most of humanity lives within one of the **24 Zones**, heavily developed regions controlled by the **Monoliths**. Outside the Zones lies the **Pale**, where conditions are much harder for humans to live in.

## GEOGRAPHY & LIFE

Zenith is a large and diverse planet. Its environments include deserts, forests, wetlands, mountains, plains, and many ecosystems with no direct Earth equivalent.

Its native life varies just as much. Some species are harmless and live alongside humans, while others can be dangerous to people and infrastructure. Unusual size, anatomy, or behavior is not considered supernatural; Zenith simply has a very different natural world from Earth.

Large portions of the planet remain sparsely explored, particularly outside developed areas.

## THE ZONES

The **24 Zones** are the main centers of human civilization on Zenith. Each is governed by a **Monolith**, which controls its government, infrastructure, and major industries.

Zones are heavily managed environments designed to keep their populations safe and comfortable. Each Zone is surrounded by a largely invisible **environmental barrier** that helps maintain its conditions and limits the movement of air, contaminants, microorganisms, and other environmental factors.

Despite the name, B eople, animals, plants, and other natural elements can cross them.

## HUMAN IMPACT

Humanity has significantly changed Zenith through pollution, resource extraction, deforestation, water diversion, and the introduction of Earth species.

Much of this history is poorly understood by the general population. Most Zenithans simply consider the environment outside the Zones to be naturally dangerous.

## SCIENCE & EXPLORATION

Despite nearly a thousand years of research, Zenith still has plenty of unanswered questions.

Its size, unfamiliar biology, difficult environments, and incomplete records from humanity's early settlement have left large parts of the planet unexplored. New species, ecosystems, and unusual environments continue to be documented.
