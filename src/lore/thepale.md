The **Pale** is the name commonly given to the regions outside the Zones. It is not a single biome or uniformly barren wasteland, but a vast collection of natural environments ranging from relatively ordinary wilderness to extremely harsh and biologically dense regions.

Parts of the Pale can look surprisingly similar to the edges of nearby Zones. The difference becomes more apparent with distance, where larger expanses of wilderness, denser vegetation, unfamiliar wildlife, and other features of Zenith's native environment become increasingly common.

The Pale can be extraordinarily beautiful, But is nevertheless dangerous for most ordinary humans due to the of environmental conditions and biological hazards.

Most Zenithans regard the Pale as inherently dangerous and unsuitable for permanent human habitation. This perception is widespread enough that the term itself carries strong negative connotations.
