[Immigration & Xenophobia]

Due to the lax immigration laws just about anyone can get into Bezel. Native Bezens use this as a loophole to hire on cheap labor from the north and south, often initially offering high pay before either “demoting” their employees, accusing them of crimes they have to pay back, or straight up kidnapping them and using them for slave labor. While this is technically illegal there’s almost no enforcement of these laws, especially outside of the big cities.

In turn almost all immigrants are assumed to be servants, or lower class. Amd while your average Bezen won’t go out of their way to discriminate against foreigners the stereotypes surrounding immigrants often does paint their interactions with them.

Economy

?

Pelf

Pelf is the official currency of Bezel, they are small thin coins made of tin with their value inscribed in the center of the coin. They are around 0.64 inches, and weigh around 0.0635 pounds. Its most notable feature is two small holes drilled into either end of every coin that can be used to sew the pelf into the lining of ones clothes for safe keeping.

The coins range from 1 , 5, 10, 50, 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 50,000, and 100,000 dollars.
