## OVERVIEW

The water of Zenith is mildly toxic. It can be safely consumed for short periods, but long-term exposure causes a variety of health problems and can significantly reduce a person's lifespan.

AquaFlo is the Monolith responsible for extracting, purifying, and distributing water across Zenith. Its infrastructure includes wells, treatment plants, pipelines, reservoirs, and countless smaller facilities scattered throughout the Zones and Pale.

AquaFlo is particularly prominent in Rho, where centuries of water extraction have made the company a major part of the Zone's economy and development.

## HISTORY

AquaFlo was established during the formation of the modern Zones, originally to deal with Zenith's contaminated water. Its early infrastructure was concentrated in what would become Rho, where the company quickly became responsible for much of the region's water supply.

As AquaFlo expanded its extraction sites, purification plants, pipelines, and reservoirs, its control over Rho's water infrastructure eventually allowed it to become the Zone's Monolith.

The company continued expanding across Zenith over the following centuries. Some of its older water sources have since been exhausted, leaving abandoned facilities scattered throughout Rho. Many have been salvaged or repurposed by locals, while others remain under AquaFlo ownership.

AquaFlo's leadership has remained within the same family for generations. 

## STRUCTURE

AquaFlo is headed by the CEO, historically titled **Lord/Lady [NAME], the Great Purifier of the Waters**. The full title still appears on official documents, although most people simply call them the CEO.

The position traditionally passes to the CEO's firstborn child. The ruling family occupies an unusually privileged position within the company, with wealthy AquaFlo families and senior management forming a loose corporate upper class beneath them.

Most employees occupy much more ordinary positions. AquaFlo employs large numbers of workers across its infrastructure, including:

- **Researchers and Hydrologists:** Study water sources and purification methods.
- **Engineers:** Design and maintain AquaFlo's infrastructure and equipment.
- **Factory Workers:** Operate treatment plants and other industrial facilities.
- **Maintenance Workers:** Repair pipes, machinery, pumps, and other equipment.
- **Surveyors and Field Workers:** Locate and assess potential water sources.
- **Security:** Protect AquaFlo facilities, workers, and valuable infrastructure.
- **Management:** Oversee individual facilities, districts, and larger operations.

## CULTURE

AquaFlo has a strong sense of hierarchy. The company's ruling family is treated with considerably more respect and ceremony than ordinary employees, and differences in wealth and living conditions are obvious.

The company takes considerable pride in its history and presents water management as a responsibility passed down through generations. Many employees genuinely take pride in keeping their communities supplied with clean water, while others are simply there for the stable employment and benefits.

Working conditions vary depending on the job. Office positions and specialized careers can be comfortable, while factory, maintenance, and field work can be physically demanding and dangerous.

AquaFlo employees often develop strong camaraderie, particularly among workers who spend long periods together at remote facilities or company towns.

## RELATIONSHIPS

### Other Monoliths

AquaFlo is not always easy to work with. Its search for new water sources occasionally brings it into other Monoliths' territory, and its leadership has a reputation for being abrasive.

Despite this, AquaFlo is too important to easily oppose. Nearly every Zone depends on its infrastructure in some capacity, and major disruptions to the water supply could affect thousands or millions of people.

### Rho

Rho has been shaped by centuries of AquaFlo extraction. Water shortages, dried vegetation, and depleted sources are common concerns in parts of the Zone, particularly around older extraction sites.

AquaFlo controls most of Rho's major water infrastructure, although independent wells, purification systems, and smaller suppliers still exist.

When a water source is no longer worth operating, AquaFlo generally abandons the facility and moves elsewhere. Equipment may be salvaged, but the structures themselves are often left behind.

Rhoans frequently repurpose these abandoned facilities as workshops, storage buildings, homes, or sources of spare parts. AquaFlo usually ignores this unless someone interferes with valuable equipment or an active system.

### The Talora

AquaFlo and the Talora have a particularly hostile relationship. AquaFlo operations have repeatedly displaced Talora communities and restricted their access to areas surrounding company infrastructure.

Talora are generally not permitted to work for AquaFlo or enter company property. Stories of more extreme actions by AquaFlo are common, although many of these remain difficult to verify.

## WORKING FOR AQUAFLO

AquaFlo employs people across nearly every level of its operations, making it possible to build very different kinds of characters around the company.

A character might work at a purification plant, maintain pipelines in a company town, or provide security for an isolated facility.

More interestingly, many employees are being forced to set up shop and survey The Pale for water sources. Working in the Pale is dangerous, but positions may come with better pay or benefits. Some employees spend years moving between facilities as the company follows new water sources.

## NOTES

- AquaFlo is one of the oldest continuously operating Monoliths.
- It is one of the most geographically spread-out Monoliths, with facilities throughout the Zones and Pale.
- AquaFlo infrastructure can remain operational for generations, with newer systems often built around older facilities.
- Many Rho settlements originally developed around AquaFlo infrastructure.
- Abandoned AquaFlo facilities are common sights throughout Rho.
