## OVERVIEW

Rho is a sparsely populated Zone dominated by a large water industry controlled by its monolith AquaFlo. Much of the Zone consists of dry plains, red sandstone, and deep canyons. Most settlements are small and separated by long stretches of undeveloped land.


## GEOGRAPHY & INFRASTRUCTURE

Rho consists primarily of red sandstone, deep canyons, mesas, dry plains, and other sun-baked terrain. Vegetation is sparse, with greener areas concentrated around settlements and reliable water sources.

Large industrial facilities are widespread throughout the Zone. Water extraction plants and other infrastructure can be enormous in scale, with the machines themselves digging into the ground almost threateningly.

Water extraction has taken place in Rho for centuries, and is one of the oldest standing monoliths. Some areas show obvious environmental decline, including drying vegetation, depleted water sources, and abandoned infrastructure. Older residents sometimes tell stories of their ancestors, when the land was much greener and had more reliable water.

Most settlements are small frontier towns, although Rho contains one major industrial and company-centered settlement. Older communities have often been incorporated into expanding industrial networks, while newer settlements were built around existing facilities.

## HISTORY

</div>

Rho's earliest human settlements were established as prisoner and slave colonies during the earliest period of settlement on Zenith. These colonies were primarily used for quarrying and extracting stone and construction materials. Over many generations, their descendants developed into the **Talora**, forming a distinct culture and identity.

Water extraction began early in Rho's history, initially alongside quarrying and other resource extraction. As exploration revealed the enormous quantities of water stored beneath the Zone, its importance increased rapidly. Growing demand from Zenith's expanding population and advances in extraction technology made increasingly large-scale exploitation possible.

Organizations controlling Rho's water resources capitalized on the discovery, expanding extraction and distribution networks throughout the Zone. The organization that would eventually become Rho's Monolith was established specifically around managing and exploiting these resources. It gradually grew into the dominant economic and political power in Rho.

As the water industry expanded, Talora communities were gradually displaced from their traditional lands by extraction facilities, pipelines, reservoirs, roads, and settlements. The process occurred over centuries rather than through a single event, leaving many Talora communities geographically and culturally separate from the rest of Rho.

Quarrying declined as water became Rho's dominant industry, although small-scale extraction remains active today. Rho has experienced periods of prosperity brought by its water industry, but its resources have been continuously exploited for much of its history, and that prosperity has never been evenly distributed.

Modern Rho remains heavily dependent on its water industry while retaining strong local communities, frontier traditions, and a culture of practical self-sufficiency.

## CULTURE

Rho has a strong regional identity. Rhoans generally take pride in their culture despite widespread stereotypes about the Zone.

The culture combines frontier traditions, Southern influences, vehicle culture, older customs, and a strong emphasis on self-sufficiency. Rhoans tend to be skeptical of outsiders and may be slow to trust people from other Zones, particularly those from wealthier or more urban areas.

Rhoans generally embrace being seen as old-fashioned. Older technology, traditional music, cooking, mechanical skills, and local customs are often preserved even when newer alternatives exist.

### (Rhoan) Appearance

Rhoans commonly have tan to medium-brown skin, brown or dark brown hair, and soft facial features with relatively broad or flat proportions. Wavy and curly hair is common, while facial hair is relatively prevalent among adults who grow it. These traits vary considerably between individuals and are not universal among Rhoans.

### Fashion

Rhoan fashion is heavily influenced by the Zone's hot, dry climate and its agricultural and industrial lifestyle. Clothing tends to be **practical, durable, and loose-fitting**, with workwear being especially common. Cargo pants, coveralls, utility vests, boots, gloves, and oversized jackets are popular, often modified with pockets, straps, tools, or other equipment. Fabrics are generally lightweight and breathable, while heavier protective clothing is worn for agricultural, mechanical, and industrial work. Bright colors, particularly **orange, red, yellow, and blue**, are common, often appearing as accents, work uniforms, or bold statement pieces.

Outside of workwear, Rhoan fashion has a strong **streetwear and motorsport influence**, with graphic jackets, racing-inspired clothing, cropped tops, loose trousers, caps, and customized garments being common. Clothing is often layered and accessorized rather than strictly coordinated, and personal modifications are encouraged. Secondhand clothing and repaired or repurposed garments are widespread, giving many outfits a worn, personalized appearance.

### Vehicle Culture

Vehicles are deeply embedded in Rhoan culture. The distance between settlements makes them necessary for many residents, while racing and vehicle modification have developed into major forms of recreation and social activity.

Rho is particularly known for heavily modified vehicles, off-road racing, and unusual custom builds. Rhoans have a reputation throughout Zenith for driving particularly extreme vehicles, although this reputation is often based more on excess and spectacle than on technical quality.

Racing takes place both informally and through organized events. Large races and vehicle shows are frequently filmed in Rho because of its open terrain.

### Music

Rho has a recognizable musical tradition that is considered older and more traditional than the music associated with many urban Zones. Its continued popularity is partly attributed to Rho's isolation and the importance placed on preserving local culture.

### Food & Drink

Rhoan cuisine is heavily inspired by (real world) Southern American comfort food. Rhoans are known for slathering their food in spices, sauces, rubs, and strong seasoning.

Fresh ingredients and home cooking are culturally important. Although grocery stores are common, many Rhoans prefer buying fresh ingredients and preparing food themselves. Small gardens, local livestock, hunting, and other small-scale food production supplement imported food depending on the settlement.

Rho also has a strong culture of locally produced alcohol. Homemade drinks are often particularly strong and pungent and can take time for outsiders to develop a taste for.

### Sports & Festivals

**Dustball** is a popular Rhoan sport loosely descended from soccer. Its rules have evolved considerably from its origins, and different communities maintain their own variations. The sport is played with a ball, although some variants use multiple balls.

Rho's largest annual festival, **the Dustrink**, combines sports, racing, food, music, drinking, and local community traditions. Major Dustball matches and races are accompanied by vehicle meets, cooking competitions, vendors, and performances.

The festival is primarily community-run. The Monolith has attempted to participate in the event in the past, but at least one major attempt was met with widespread booing and extremely poor attendance. Since then, Monolith involvement has generally remained limited.

### Local Speech

Rhoan speech has its own regional slang.

- **Longhaul** – A long-distance trip, particularly between distant settlements.
- **Tin** – An old vehicle or piece of machinery.
- **Dry luck** – Bad luck or an unfortunate situation.
- **Company town** – A settlement dominated by Monolith infrastructure and employment.
- **Pipe Rat** – Someone who works around or for the water Monolith. It can also be used dismissively toward Monolith employees.
- **Pipe Mouse** – The child of a Pipe Rat, or someone who grew up around the Monolith without working for it.
- **Dusting** – Driving or riding around without a particular destination.
- **Burnout** – Someone who spends most of their money on gambling, racing, and related hobbies.
- **Junkblood** – A self-proclaimed title used by people who build, repair, or modify things from scrap.

Some terms are also used against Rhoans by outsiders. ***Rusty***, ***Canyon Rat***, ***Tanker***, and ***Outback*** are derogatory terms associated with stereotypes of Rhoans as poor, isolated, or backwards.

## DAILY LIFE

Most Rhoans live in small settlements rather than large cities. Daily life generally revolves around work, family, local businesses, and community activities.

Practical maintenance is a normal part of life. Residents commonly repair vehicles, machinery, buildings, and household goods rather than replacing them. Mechanics, fabricators, and other skilled tradespeople are consequently important members of many communities.

Water availability varies between settlements. Larger cities and industrial communities generally have reliable supplies, while remote communities may depend on wells, storage systems, or water deliveries. Conservation is more important in poorer and isolated areas.

Food is supplemented by local gardens, livestock, and hunting in some communities. Bars, diners, garages, shops, and private homes commonly serve as gathering places.

Education is generally less well-funded than in wealthier Zones. Rho has several trade schools and one university in its main industrial area, but residents seeking specialized education often leave the Zone.

Fixing is also particularly common. Rho's isolation and limited law-enforcement coverage make it a common starting point and temporary base for Fixers.

## SUBZONES / DISTRICTS

Rho is administratively divided into several broad districts. Unlike densely populated Zones, many of these divisions contain large amounts of undeveloped land.

### Industrial District

The primary administrative and industrial center of Rho. It contains the largest concentration of Monolith offices, corporate infrastructure, major water facilities, and industrial employment, as well as its own *(relatively small in comparison to other zones)* city.

### Badlands District

The largest and least-developed part of the Zone, consisting primarily of barren wilderness, abandoned infrastructure, remote roads, and isolated water sources.

More remote areas contain settlements, isolated infrastructure, mines, workshops, and long stretches of undeveloped terrain.

Monolith services and Gavel presence are more limited here.

### Historical District

The districts containing Rho's established towns and communities. These include both older settlements and newer communities developed around industrial facilities.

## KEY LOCATIONS

- **The Industrial District** – Rho's largest concentration of corporate infrastructure, industry, businesses, and employment.
- **Major Water Facilities** – Large extraction, purification, storage, and distribution complexes operated by the Rhoan Monolith.
- **Frontier Towns** – Numerous small settlements spread throughout the Zone.
- **Dustball Grounds** – Local and regional sporting grounds found throughout Rho.
- **Racing Grounds** – Organized tracks and open areas used for professional and amateur racing.

## ECONOMY

Water extraction and distribution form the foundation of Rho's economy. The Rhoan Monolith operates the majority of the Zone's large-scale water infrastructure and supplies water to other Zones.

Other industries include mining, quarrying, construction, transportation, freight, salvage, fabrication, repair, and other trades. Quarrying was historically more important before the expansion of the water industry.

Economic conditions vary considerably between communities. Areas connected to major industrial operations generally have better infrastructure and employment opportunities, while remote settlements have fewer services and greater dependence on local resources.

Despite its economic importance to Zenith, Rho has significant poverty and inequality. The wealth generated by the water industry does not reach all communities equally.

## HOOKS & RUMORS

- Some remote communities claim their local water sources are becoming unreliable despite official assurances.
- Old settlements contain abandoned wells, roads, industrial equipment, and other infrastructure from earlier periods of development.
- Some Rhoans believe the Zone is becoming steadily less habitable, although the change is slow enough that most people barely notice it.
- Independent mechanics and fabricators sometimes build unusual vehicles from salvaged industrial equipment.
- Remote settlements occasionally disappear as residents leave after losing reliable access to water or employment.
- Fixers frequently use isolated Rhoan towns as temporary bases between jobs.
- The relationship between local communities and the Monolith remains tense despite Rho's dependence on its water infrastructure.

## TRIVIA

- Rho is the largest source of processed water for the rest of Zenith.
- Vehicle and racing programs frequently use Rho as a filming location because of its open terrain.
- Some Rhoan communities maintain buildings and machinery that have been repaired or modified for generations.
- Locally produced alcohol is often considered unusually strong by people from other Zones.
- Rho's major annual festival has historically remained independent of Monolith sponsorship.
- Rhoans generally have a higher degree of practical mechanical and self-sufficiency skills than outsiders expect.
